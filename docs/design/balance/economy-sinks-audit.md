# Economy sinks and recoverability audit

Scope: authored R1/R2 gold-facing content and implemented transaction semantics at `rpg-sim@1.1.0`. The source snapshot is `c9531ec6b68c0c6c3213c3d07ce39a68767c92c931a11986b6e1f51a295488f3` (compiled content hash recorded in [region-2-content.md](../region-2-content.md)); this labels the content snapshot, not an economy measurement. Historical statements below describe that snapshot and its prior no-sell runtime.

## What the runtime does

- Gold enters through debug/admin grants and gameplay rewards as distinct paths: debug grants are operator tooling, while live reward effects and idle/expedition loot settlement are gameplay. Idle combat grants rolled enemy loot; gather activities grant their authored resource yield loot tables (`games/rpg/packages/rpg-sim/src/expedition/simulate.ts`). Server settlement applies those deltas through the ledger (`games/rpg/apps/server/src/expeditions/service.ts`). These are sources, not observed per-hour rates.
- The only authored player-facing gold sink in the source snapshot is merchant purchase. Region 1 prices are 12 gold/minor healing potion, 18/sap tonic, and 40/healing potion; the last offer requires Region 1 completion. Stock is infinite (`games/rpg/content/merchants/region_1.json`). The server multiplies unit price by quantity and debits gold from inventory atomically with granting stackable consumables; mutation requires sanctuary (`games/rpg/apps/server/src/inventory/service.ts`). In that prior runtime no merchant sells equipment and no item sell command/value-to-gold conversion exists; `Item.value` is not then realizable gold.
- Other ledger movement (inventory/storage/equip, expedition escrow/return, live run bag/corpse) changes containers, not total gold. Expedition starting escrow concerns consumables, not gold (`games/rpg/apps/server/src/expeditions/service.ts`).

## Auditable content comparisons

R1 authored direct gold rolls: mite 0–2; boar 1–4; creeper 2–5; lantern 3–7; tiller 5–10; Matriarch 40–60 (`games/rpg/content/loot-tables/region_1.json`). Those bounds are per enemy loot-table roll before any death bag loss; encounter composition and kill rates determine totals and are not modeled here. At R1 merchant prices (12 minor potion, 18 sap tonic, 40 healing potion), the Matriarch's 40–60 gold range corresponds arithmetically to 3–5 minor potions (floor(40/12)=3; floor(60/12)=5), 2–3 tonics, or 1 healing potion. These are price comparisons, not a claim a boss encounter funds those purchases. Gathering loot tables currently have no direct gold.

R2 authored direct gold ranges are glass echo 2–5, prism guard 4–8, and Warden 50–70 (`games/rpg/content/loot-tables/region_2.json`). The Warden's single direct gold roll is enough at R1 prices for 4–5 minor potions, 2–3 tonics, or 1 healing potion; prices are comparisons, not a progression or purchase claim. The R2 gathering resource-yield tables have no direct gold. `item:prism_dust`'s value 5 is not a sell price (`games/rpg/content/items/region_2.json`).

## Death, loss, and XP semantics

- Idle natural-end death: 50% of each positive bag grant is removed using integer floor of loss, so an odd unit is retained; this covers newly earned gold/items, not starting holdings or consumed escrow (`games/rpg/packages/rpg-sim/src/expedition/settle.ts`). Cancellation/member-left and cutoffs before the fatal segment do not apply that penalty. The gold/item bag loss applies when the settlement cutoff covers the fatal segment; XP penalty applies only to heroes recorded as having died, not every hero in the expedition. For each such hero, XP is awarded from credited kills, then their total (prior XP plus earned XP) loses 10% of the XP requirement for that level, floored, capped at available total; deleveling is possible, level 1 zero floor applies. Thus report `net = earned - penaltyLost` is a signed change over the run; it can be negative and is not the actual post-run XP total (`games/rpg/packages/rpg-sim/src/expedition/settle.ts`, `games/rpg/packages/rpg-stats/src/xp.ts`).
- Live death uses a distinct rule: the hero loses 10% of the current level’s next-level requirement (not 10% of all accumulated XP), floor/capped at current total. Items and gold in that instance’s run bag move to a corpse, recoverable at the same area/location before the 24-hour expiry. Expiry, a subsequent death invalidating an older corpse, or other sink path destroys those holdings; no gold fee is charged (`games/rpg/apps/server/src/live/effects.ts`).
- **Update (#129, 2026-11-04):** the two XP bullets above describe the audited pre-#129 behavior. Live death and idle settlements started under `rpg-idle@2.1.0` now use the level-scaled rule `lossBp(L) = max(200, 1000 − 20 × (L − 1))` of total XP with a one-level cap (open decision #8); expeditions already running under `rpg-idle@2.0.0` still settle with the old rule. Item and gold loss are unchanged.

## Measurement limits / next experiment

Current feature note: `inventory.sellMaterial` now lets an actor sell only banked, stackable materials with positive authored integer `Item.value`, at that unchanged value per unit. The server applies stack debit and inventory gold credit in one ledger batch at Sanctuary. This enables material value realization; the historical content comparisons and balance-sim limitations below remain unchanged. No economy measurement or final balance claim is made here.

The balance-sim hourly section does not model the economy: its historical model prices drops at item `value` as “vendorValue,” assumes a live route of 36 fights/hour and 6 potions/hour, while idle hourly runs use synthetic budget/regen/potion assumptions; that model predates material sales and does not execute the new ledger transaction. Idle death XP loss is also excluded from gross XP/h (`games/rpg/tools/balance-sim/README.md`, `src/loot.ts`, `src/simulate.ts`). It therefore cannot establish realized gold/hour, purchase affordability, net gold flow, or death-adjusted XP. The smallest useful next experiment is a seeded server-settlement harness recording actual ledger gold deltas, material sales, and purchases for a declared activity/cohort/duration, with paired death and survival cases; report sample-level outcomes and inputs, not extrapolated hours.
